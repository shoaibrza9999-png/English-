The Lion and the Woodcutter

Book I: Mitrabheda

In a dense forest far beyond the reach of the bustling kingdoms, there lived a lion of immense strength and majesty. He ruled over the thickets and clearings with a quiet authority that commanded the respect of every creature, from the smallest beetle to the largest elephant.

One afternoon, while the sun filtered through the canopy in long, golden shafts of light, a weary woodcutter stumbled upon the lion’s den. He was not looking for trouble, but for a rare wood that was said to possess a fragrance so sweet it could calm even the most troubled heart.

The lion watched him from the shadows, his eyes amber like old honey. The woodcutter froze, his axe trembling in his hand. But the beast did not roar. Instead, he stepped forward, the soft padding of his paws nearly silent on the moss-covered earth.
